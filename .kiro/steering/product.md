# Product steering

Spectre Scribe is a web app where multiple agents write a novel in relay.
The user supplies a sample passage (their own writing or public-domain text only).
The app first computes style statistics from the sample, then builds a story bible,
then writes chapter by chapter with a continuity agent checking every draft.

Scope for the demo: one science-fiction short story, 3 chapters. No desktop build,
no long-form generation, no crawling of commercial novel sites.
